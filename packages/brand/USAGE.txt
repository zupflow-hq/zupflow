COLORS (sRGB)
Ink / near-black: #10191D — RGB 16, 25, 29
Cyan: #35C8F5 — RGB 53, 200, 245
White: #FFFFFF — RGB 255, 255, 255
Off-white presentation background: #F5F8FA — RGB 245, 248, 250
Use ink or cyan+ink on light surfaces. Use white or cyan+white on dark
surfaces. All-cyan is best on dark backgrounds; avoid it for small wordmarks
on white. White transparent files need a dark background to be visible.

USAGE
SVG is preferred for websites, design tools and print: it scales cleanly.
All logo letters are outlined vector paths, so no font install is required.
PNG files are transparent except the deliberately opaque square tile files.
Use a PNG at least twice its displayed width for high-density screens.
ICO files embed 16, 32 and 48px images. Browser/OS masking should be applied
to the square tiles by the platform; do not bake your own rounded corners.

SPACING AND SIZE (recommended starting points)
Keep outside clear space of at least 16 units per 100-unit icon canvas
(about one loop-stroke thickness). The exported artboards include padding,
but do not always include the full recommended external clear space.
For wordmark-only use, clear space should be at least half the x-height.
Recommended minimum displayed widths: icon 24px, horizontal 160px,
stacked 120px, wordmark 120px. 16px icons are supplied specifically for
favicons; at that size fine separation is naturally less distinct.
For print, start at icon 8mm, horizontal 40mm, stacked/wordmark 30mm and
proof at final size. Colors are RGB; ask your printer for CMYK conversion
using the actual press/paper profile rather than assuming a universal CMYK.

PRESERVE THE DESIGN
The exact chosen B1 angular loop, separate play triangle and original
lowercase outlined wordmark are retained. The horizontal lockup preserves
the original placement. Stacked and wordmark-only are new arrangements of
the same paths. Do not stretch, rotate individual elements, round corners,
change the play triangle, add effects, or retype the wordmark.
