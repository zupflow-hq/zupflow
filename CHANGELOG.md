# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (alpha) — 2026-10-24

The first public release.

- The API server: organizations, isolated workspaces per customer, apps through MCP, hosted Connect, agents with granted tools, a tool policy with approvals, durable runs, signed webhooks and an audit log, with Sandbox and Production kept apart.
- The Console, for your team to configure and watch all of it.
- `@zupflow/sdk` and `@zupflow/contract` on npm.
- Documentation at [docs.zupflow.com](https://docs.zupflow.com).
